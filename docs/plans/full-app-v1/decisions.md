# Full App v1: decisions and assumptions

The owner asked (18 Nov 2026) for the whole [Full App Specification](../../spec/NabaLens-Full-App-Specification-v1.0.md) to be built autonomously, choosing the recommended option for routine decisions and recording it. Each decision below says what was chosen, why, and what would reverse it. "Spec §" points at the specification; "D8", "D9" and "DESIGN.md" at [the Album design system](../../design-system/DESIGN.md).

Owner review is wanted on the ones marked **review**.

## Model choices

| ID    | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Why                                                                   |

| FA-30 | No rebuild of the existing per-kind tables (menu photos, area photos, amenity checks, knowledge). | FA-31 | Each review decision has a level: **public** (closes a public-photo requirement) or **reference** (kept, does not close it).                                                                                                        | Spec §2, invariant 4, AC16.                                           |
| FA-32 | Amenity questions have a photo requirement separate from the fact.
