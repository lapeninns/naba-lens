## Decisions made (for the coordinator to record)

1. **R3-1:** A matched photo is bound to the venue it was matched for and is never reserved at another venue (`p_venue_id` sent from the client). After a venue switch, photos still waiting their turn are taken back and asked again. Photos whose reservation started finish at the old venue.
2. **R3-2:** On a staff Match photo, Waiting means the label is on the photo (finalise `sent_for_review`, or a save answered `"saved"`). Until then it reads Sent.
3. **R3-3:** Match holds back only slots this phone is still sending. Waiting slots stay offered (§6 S1).
4. **R3-4:** Offline end words: "N photo(s) saved on this phone" / "They send when the signal returns."
5. **R3-5:** Match later is scoped to the active venue.
6. **R3-6:** Staff "Asked for" opens Add with `route: "request"` (S2-METRICS request route).

No `departures.md` or `catalogue.md` rows were needed.
