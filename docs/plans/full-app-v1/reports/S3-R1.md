## Decisions for the coordinator to record

1. **Unlabelled rule (K1).** A ready, undeleted general upload that no slot holds (no dish label, no live dish row, no live place label, no amenity, knowledge or checklist link), not set aside, and one of: an open I'm not sure request with no label saved (`not_sure`); a food or drink tag with no dish (`no_dish`); no label saved and 7 days old (`unmatched`, which covers "Match later over 7 days" and "no label at all"; videos too, which Match does not take). A fresh Match later photo stays with its person (FA-4) and is not unlabelled.
2. **Library meaning.** `labels=unlabelled` is the rule for admins. In a staff member's own library it stays "no label saved" (Me's rule D-12), because `library_page` also serves staff.
3. **Manager Overview count** applies the Review queue's visibility (not the manager's own photo, leavers' photos admin only), so the number equals the list it links to.
4. **Gate and search.** Widening `menu_photo_unlabelled_eligible` is what lets a manager open an I'm not sure or 7-day photo. `asset_search`'s prefilter P must hold for every row the gate admits, so P gains a cheap superset term (a help request, or no saved label and 7 days old).
5. **V17 shape.** Place restore closes the label and re-adds it (labels have one live row per photo and an append-only decision log). Amenity restore appends a `pending` decision.
6. **V15 placement.** A trigger, not a check inside each function, so any dish writer is covered.
7. **K6.** Admins keep their old rules (they may decide their own dish photo; nobody decides their own place or amenity photo). The spot-check list shows every decision made on one's own photo at the venue, admins' included.
8. **K11.** The filter is photo or video. Videos appear only as Needs label. A manager cannot open a staff video's preview: the gate is still images only, and video posters are not built (S12.3).
