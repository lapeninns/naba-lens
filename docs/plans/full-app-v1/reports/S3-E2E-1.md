## Product defects

Screenshots are in `.artifacts/s3-e2e-1/` in this worktree (git-ignored).

**D1 (blocker): staff Album Home never loads.** `app/staff/tasks/page.tsx` (server) calls `askedForItems` from `components/nabalens/requests/staff-requests.tsx`, which is a `"use client"` module. The server logs "Attempted to call askedForItems() from the server but askedForItems is on the client".

- Steps: sign in as any staff or manager and open `/staff/tasks`.
- Expected: Album.
- Actual: "We couldn't load Home". Every staff sign-in lands there.
- Specs: all of `full-app/` except AC13; app-bar-fit; auth-return (staff Log out).
- Fix: move `askedForItems` to a non-client module, for example `lib/requests/`.
- Screenshots: `D1-home-fails-real-head.png`, `D1-app-bar-fit-manager-home.png`.

**D6 (high): grouped photos get each other's labels.** This hit 17 of 27 AC05 runs plus AC37, at every size.

- Steps: Add photos with 5 photos. Choose Photo 2 too, then Soup. Choose Photo 4 too, then Pie. Choose Garden.
- Expected: photos 1 and 2 are Soup; photos 3 and 4 are Pie.
- Actual: for example soup-1 is Pie, soup-2 Soup, pie-1 Soup, pie-2 Pie (`media_label_items`). The UI end screen correctly shows Soup × 2, Pie × 2. The stored bytes match each file's name (a SHA-256 check in the spec), so the labels are crossed between assets, not the files. The place label is always right.
- In AC37, an "I'm not sure" photo took a grouped photo's place, leaving that photo with no label.
- Spec: `ac05-batch-groups.spec.ts`.
- Screenshots: `D6-ac05-summary-ui-correct.png`, `D6-ac37-phone.png`.

**D9 (high, confirms K1): I'm not sure doesn't reach the manager.**

- Steps: Match, search "lamb shank" (no result), add a note, I'm not sure; then the manager opens `/manager/review`.
- Expected: the photo is in the main queue as Needs label, with the note.
- Actual: "Nothing to review". Me also lists the photo under Needs attention as "No slot yet … under Match later", so it seems to be stored as Match later.
- Spec: `ac03-needs-label.spec.ts`.
- Screenshots: `D9-ac03-manager-review-empty.png`, `D9-ac03-staff-summary.png`.

**D8 (medium): Match ignores a venue switch made in another tab.**

- Steps: a person is staff at A and B. Open Match at A with 3 photos and answer photo 1 as Soup. In another tab, Me → Change venue to B. Answer photos 2 and 3.
- Expected: Match says "You are now adding photos for B" and offers B's slots (the code has `revalidateVenue` for this).
- Actual: Match keeps "For A" and A's slots. The photos went to B, and the end says "3 photos for A".
- No old-venue label survived (`foreignLabels` = 0), but labels were mixed as in D6: photo 1, answered Soup, ended at B with no label; photo 3, answered Lager, ended at A labelled Soup.
- Spec: `ac11-ac14-access.spec.ts`.
- Screenshots: `D8-match-after-switch.png`, `D8-match-summary.png`.

**D2 (medium): an admin's deep link is lost at the MFA step.**

- Steps: signed out, open `/admin/staff?from=email`, sign in by link and pass the code.
- Expected: `/admin/staff?from=email`.
- Actual: `/admin`.
- Spec: `auth-return.spec.ts` (AUTH-1).
- Screenshot: `D2-admin-deep-link-lost.png`.

**D3 (low): admin AppBar is 4 px short at 1280 and 1440.** The seven links end at 892 and the right-hand group starts at 912, short of the required spacing.

- Specs: `app-bar-fit.spec.ts`, `responsive.spec.ts`.
- Screenshot: `D3-admin-appbar-1280.png`.

**D4 (low): wrong wording after FA-20.** The task screen's sent notice says "They're already in your portfolio and Explore. Review only decides whether they count." (`components/nabalens/tasks/submit-photos-panel.tsx:724`). Colleagues no longer see photos before review.

- Spec: `coverage-staff.spec.ts` (soft checks).
- Screenshot: `D4-sent-notice-explore.png`.

**D7 (low): offline end screen overclaims.**

- Steps: go offline after Match loads, then answer Salad.
- Actual: the row says "Saved on this phone" (correct), but the heading says "1 slot filled" and "Each one shows as Waiting in the album". Nothing has reached the server. The album tile says "saved on this phone", and the photo sends once back online.
- Related: offline before Match opens, the slots can't load ("The empty slots could not be loaded"); only Match later is possible.
- Spec: `ac09-ac10-offline-gallery.spec.ts`.
- Screenshot: `D7-offline-summary.png`.

**D5 (low): Recent skips a slot used earlier in the same visit.** After matching Salad and returning to the album without a reload, the next Match doesn't list Salad under Recent. After a reload it does, as "Waiting · another photo is welcome".

- Spec: `ac02-quick-upload.spec.ts`.
- Screenshot: `D5-recent-same-visit.png`.
