## Decisions for the coordinator to record

- **R4-1 (V5):** Change slot is one database call, `move_my_upload_slot`. It runs the existing label RPCs inside one transaction, so their checks (venue, catalogue, retired items or places, withdrawn area credit) still apply, and any refusal leaves the old slot.
- **R4-2 (V13):** Change slot is offered only for a photo sent to the active venue, with a hint to switch venue. I did not add a board for another venue, because `loadMatchBoardAction` (`app/staff/_shell/match-actions.ts`) belongs to S3-R3. If a cross-venue board is wanted later, the change is to give `loadMatchBoardAction(venueId?)` a membership check, then have Me pass `item.venueId` and drop the hint.
- **R4-3 (V11):** a dish photo that needs a recheck reads to staff as its accepted level ("In the album" / "Kept for reference"). It does not get a separate staff word.
- **R4-4 (K4):** Label corrected counts as accepted on Me (Ask to remove, Ask for a correction; never Withdraw or Change slot).
- **R4-5 (E8):** a retake is answered only by an own photo of the same slot sent after the retake was asked.
- **R4-6 (E4):** an I'm-not-sure photo with no label stays under Needs attention and reads "With your manager, who will choose its slot."
