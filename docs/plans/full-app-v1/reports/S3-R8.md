## Decisions for the coordinator to record

1. **R8-1 (W2):** Needs label photos are listed on Review whatever the menu state. Choosing a dish needs a ready menu; Not used, Mark duplicate and the place and facility routes on the photo detail do not. Overview's count keeps no menu gate.
2. **R8-2 (W1):** A Needs label video offers no dish pick on the Review list. Its card says to open the details and mark it Not used.
3. **R8-3 (W4):** With Videos chosen, Review leaves out the place and amenity sections, because their queues hold photos only. Photos, or no media filter, keeps them.
4. **R8-4 (open):** The admin Review page (`/admin/coverage/[venueId]/menu-photos`) at a venue with no ready menu shows the menu-pending notice only, not the Needs label list. Admins have no photo-detail route there (`photoHref` is null for the admin scope), so a listed photo would lead nowhere. This sits next to V18 (the admin page does not mount `AmenityReviewSection`). Both need an admin photo detail, or links to the Library.
5. **R8-5 (open):** `label_help_queue` admits any user whose `venue_role_of` is `manager`, including a staff account managing by a legacy grant (FA-22; tested in `label-help.test.ts`). The Review queue's Needs label branch and Overview's count also require `private.menu_photo_can_review_unlabelled()` (`is_active_manager()` or admin), which is false for that account. So a legacy staff-held grant sees Needs label photos in the help list but none on Review. I left the gate alone: aligning it either breaks FA-22 or widens who may label (`menu_photo_unlabelled_eligible`), and that needs a product decision.
