Generated from the gap map of 18 Nov 2026 (11 area readers plus a completeness critic, read-only, against `feat/full-app-v1`), then corrected by the critic's spot checks. Every requirement in the [spec](../../spec/NabaLens-Full-App-Specification-v1.0.md) has a row: its status at the start, the sprint task that owns it ([sprint-plan.md](sprint-plan.md)), and the acceptance check that proves it. Rows already **done** at the start are owned by Sprint 3 for verification only. Conflicts with earlier owner decisions are resolved in [decisions.md](decisions.md).

Totals at the start: 493 requirements: 125 done, 224 partial, 102 missing, 32 conflict, 10 Release 2.

Result column: filled in as each sprint lands (built, verified, deferred, blocked) with the commit.
