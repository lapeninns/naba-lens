The page frame and BackLink

Every manager and admin page is built from the same five parts, in this order. The shell moves into a layout, so loading and error states keep the nav.

  • 1. NavSideNav from 1100px · tabs 768 to 1099px · TabBar below

    One level. The current item is bottle text on bottle-soft with aria-current="page". Counts sit beside Review and Album.

  • 2. VenueBarEvery manager page; every venue-scoped admin page

    The venue’s full name, the person’s role there, and Change venue only when they have more than one. Cross-venue admin pages say “All venues”.

  • 3. BackLinkDetail pages only

    A chevron and the parent’s name: “Menu”, “Requests”, “People”. Never “Back to”, never “Home”. It is a real link to the parent, with its filters kept; it does not call history back.

  • 4. TitleOne Fraunces display heading

    Names what the page is about. Status chips and one summary line sit under it. The one bottle button sits to its right on desktop and under it, full width, on phones.

  • 5. Section tabsOnly where a section has siblings

    Label type, the current tab in ink over a 3px bottle bar. Each tab is its own route, so it can be linked, loaded and fail on its own.

BackLink, in code

One component replaces the five builders the audit found (backHref, reviewHref, requestHrefs, amenityHrefs and the back props). It takes href and label, renders an <a> above the title, and the label is always the title of the page it opens. The “Back link” column of each route map is its content.

Manager

Five sections, as today. What changes is that each word means one thing and nothing is left outside the nav.

Problems fixed

  • “Album” meant four thingsAlbum is now only the venue’s album of slots, with three tabs: Menu, Spaces, Amenities. “Album places” becomes Places and briefs, “Staff Album” becomes Posted by staff under Review, and the second “Places” page is the Spaces tab.
  • Loading and errors said “Overview” everywhereThe shell is a layout, and Album, Exports, Your photos and Account get their own boundaries. Each names its own page and keeps the nav.
  • Two copies of the same screenOne Your photos list (the shared Me component) and one amenity fallback. The manager-only copies go.
  • Team, metrics, search, uploads and account had no nav entryTeam and Progress are Venue tabs. Search opens from Album. Account and Your photos open from the avatar in the venue bar.
  • The “not a manager” page named old tabsIt names no tabs. It says what the account can open and links there.
  • No venue bar on review screensVenueBar is on every manager screen, above the title.
  • Overview, Venue and Team were still Common RoomAll three are redrawn on v2 below.

Navigation

  • Overview/manager

    What needs the manager today, as one list. Each row opens the place where the work is done. Nothing lives only here.

  • ReviewOne at a time · All photos · Posted by staff

    Every photo decision. The count beside it is photos waiting.

  • AlbumMenu · Spaces · Amenities

    Every slot the venue has and what state it is in. Gaps first. The count beside it is amenity findings to confirm.

  • RequestsList · new · one request

    Asking staff for a shot, and shot lists.

  • VenueDetails · Team · Progress · Exports · History

    What is true about the venue, who works there, how the month is going, what has been exported and what has changed.

Problems fixed

  • Nothing linked to Albums or Knowledge; Metrics only from CoverageAlbums, This month (today’s Coverage) and Metrics are tabs of Overview. The four knowledge pages are the Venue facts tab of Settings, under plain names.
  • Knowledge and Metrics lit Overview by accidentEach page lights the section it now sits in: Metrics lights Overview, Venue facts lights Settings, Venue managers lights People.

Loading, empty and error

The same three patterns on every manager and admin page. All three keep the venue bar, the title and the nav. There are no toasts: a result is written where the action was.

Interaction states

These are live: hover, press and tab through them.

In the albumWaitingRetakeMissingFailed or unknown

Hover moves the ground, never the text: outlined controls take surface-hover, the bottle button takes bottle-strong. Focus is the 2px ink outline at 2px offset. Selected is an ink fill with a tick. Disabled keeps its label and gives its reason in a caption. In forced colours every chip, button and count gets a system border and icons draw in CanvasText.

Decisions and reasons

The owner was away, so these were chosen as defaults. Each is small to reverse.

No URL changes

Only titles, tabs, the lit section and back links move. That keeps bookmarks, emails and tests working and makes R4 and R5 a shell change, not a migration.

Album keeps the nav word; three other screens are renamed

Staff already call the slot grid Album. Managers get the same thing with the same three shelves: Menu, Spaces, Amenities.

“Staff Album” becomes “Posted by staff”, a Review tab

It is a feed of what staff posted, and what a manager does there is review. Calling it an album was the fourth meaning.

One pair of decision words: Keep in the album, Ask for another photo

Today the quick screen says Accept and Ask again while the list says Approve for public use and Ask for another photo. The quick screen now uses the words that match the chips staff then see: In the album, Retake. The detail page keeps the finer decisions (Approve for public use, Keep as reference, Not used).

Filters use the chip words

Album filters read Missing, Waiting, Retake, In the album, All. “Has a photo” and “Still needs a photo” go, so a filter and the chip it selects say the same thing.

Request “Open” is a neutral chip

Nothing has happened yet. Submitted is progress, Blocked is attention, Completed is positive.

One category set: Food, Drinks, Spaces, Amenities

Admin tables said Facilities in one place and Amenities and Areas in another. The manager’s Album tabs set the words.

A venue’s coverage pages light Venues

Overview compares venues; Venues is where you work on one. The per-venue pages keep their URLs under /admin/coverage and join Setup as tabs of the same venue.

Two admin review screens become one tab with a pick

They review different things (menu and space photos; checklist photos), so both stay. They share a title, a tab and a count.

Knowledge is called Venue facts

No admin says “knowledge”. Runs become Imports and Schema upgrades become Question changes. It sits in Settings because it is rare, group-wide set-up work.

Venue managers moves to People

It is a list of people and their access. It lit Overview only because its URL starts with /admin/coverage.

Section headings are Figtree

The rule is one Fraunces display heading per screen. The Fraunces title style stays for sheets and dialogs only; big counts keep the figure style.

Codes are one field, not six boxes

The sign-in code is 6 to 10 digits depending on set-up, and one field pastes and autofills more reliably.

Retry, not Try again

Retry is one of the fixed words in the guidelines and the brief. Every error notice uses it.

The page title is the photo’s label

“Photo details” and “Photo review” name the page type. The slot or label says which photo it is.

Left for the owner

Does Keep in the album mean approved for public use?

The quick screen’s Accept puts the photo in the album today. If public use needs its own decision, the button label stays and the detail page carries the rest.

Nine venues or ten?

The brief says nine. The repo’s notes say ten are active. The overview table draws nine; nothing in the design depends on the number.

Venue names

Only The Old Crown, Girton; The White Horse Inn, Swavesey; and The White Horse, Waterbeach are named in full in the repo. The other rows use names from the venue sites, without a town where I could not confirm one.

Sample data

Counts, people, dates and most dishes are examples. Momo, Chicken Masala and Himali Lamb come from the brief; the dish photographs are the repo’s own guide images.

What was checked

Every route under app/manager, app/admin, app/login and app/auth has a row in a route map. Screens use only the values in app/globals.css, light and dark. Status words are from status-chip.tsx. Each drawn screen has one display heading and at most one bottle button. Controls are 40px on desktop frames and 48px on phone frames.

Not checked: the drawings are static, so 320px, 200% zoom and screen-reader behaviour still need testing in the app. Nothing in the NabaLens repo was changed.
