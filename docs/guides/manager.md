# Naba-Lens for venue managers

A short guide to a manager's jobs at one venue. The words in **bold** are the words on your screen. For the full reference, see the older [manager guide](../usage-manager.md). Your staff's side is in the [staff guide](staff.md).

## The five tabs

**Overview · Review · Album · Requests · Venue.** **Add photos** sits in the header. Overview's **Do next** panel lists what is waiting, starting with **Review next · _n_ photos waiting**.

## Four different things

Keep these apart when you read a number or answer a question from staff.

| Stage     | Words you see                                                                              | What it means                                                                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sent      | **Waiting**, "Uploaded … · waiting …"                                                      | Staff sent the photo. Nobody has decided yet. It doesn't count towards the album.                                                              |
| Decided   | **Approve for public use**, **Keep as reference**, **Ask for another photo**, **Not used** | Your decision on one photo. Staff see **In the album**, **Kept for reference**, **Retake** or **Not used**.                                    |
| Covered   | "_n_ of _N_", with the percentage beside it                                                | A slot counts as covered only with a current photo accepted for public use. A reference photo, an extra photo or a duplicate never adds to it. |
| Published | **Exported**, **Published**                                                                | Exported means someone downloaded a package. Published means an admin recorded the page address, date and who checked it.                      |

## Review photos

1. Open **Review**. Photos come one at a time, oldest first ("_n_ photos to review").
2. Choose **Accept** to put it in the album, or **Ask again** and pick what the next photo should fix (for example **Too dark**, **Out of focus**, **Not how it's served now**). Use **Other** to write your own reason.
3. **Next photo** moves on. **See every photo as a list** shows the whole queue with filters such as **Waiting 7+ days** and **Possible duplicates**.

For any other decision, open **Details, Fix label and more decisions**. The photo page shows where it came from, who took it and its history, and offers:

- **Approve for public use**: it fills its slot and staff see **In the album**.
- **Keep as reference**: the photo is kept for the team but is not public, and the slot stays open.
- **Ask for another photo**: the person sees **Retake** with your reason.
- **Fix label**: see below.
- **Not used**, or **Mark duplicate** for the same file sent twice. A duplicate never counts twice.

To decide several at once, use **Decide several** in the list, choose the photos, then approve, keep as reference or mark them not used.

You can't decide on your own photo: it reads "This is your photo. Another reviewer decides on it."

If two of you decide the same photo, the second sees "Someone else decided this photo first", and the first decision stands.

### Fix a label without a new photo

When staff matched a good photo to the wrong dish, don't ask for a retake. Open the photo's details and choose **Fix label**, then the right menu item. The photo is accepted for that item in the same step, the staff member's label is kept in the history, and they see **Label corrected**. Nobody has to send the photo again.

### Photos that need a label

Photos sent with **I'm not sure** carry **Needs label**, and the list's **Unlabelled photos** view gathers them. Open one and use **Fix label** to choose the item.

## Ask for photos (Requests)

1. Open **Requests** and choose **New request**.
2. Start **From a slot**, from a **New brief**, or as a **Fact check**.
3. Write the **Brief**: one line on what the photo must show. Staff read it on the slot.
4. Optionally set **Needed by**. It orders the slot for staff and never turns overdue. **More detail** holds **Priority**, team, orientation and more.
5. Tap **Send the request**.

Staff see it on Album under **Asked for**. A request on a slot completes when its photo is accepted. Requests read **Open**, **Submitted**, **Completed**, **Blocked** or **Cancelled**. Cancelling or blocking one asks for a reason. **Shot lists** group a few requests, for example for a menu launch.

## Keep the venue right (Venue)

- **To confirm**: say whether each fact or space photo is **Still right**. Only the date it was checked changes; no new photo is needed.
- **Dishes, availability and local names**: **Mark not available** keeps the dish's slot and photos while it is off; **Presentation changed** sends its accepted photos back for a recheck; **Ask for a permanent change** goes to an admin.
- **Local specials**: dated dishes and drinks. Staff can suggest them; you accept or decline.
- **Album places and briefs**: where staff photograph each section, and a one-line brief.
- **Facilities and questions**: the amenity questions and staff findings to confirm.
- **Team**: **Invite staff** and remove staff. Only an admin can make someone a manager.
- **Review assignments**: the main and backup reviewer. An admin names the main reviewer.
- **History**: every recorded change at the venue.

## Export photos

1. From Overview or Venue, open **Export photos**, then **Make an export package**.
2. **Choose photos**. Only photos accepted for public use now can be chosen.
3. Check the **Alt text**, pick an **Output size** and say **What it's for**, then **Make package · _n_ photos**.
4. **Download ZIP**. The package now reads **Exported**.

Exported is not Published. When the photos go on a website or listing, an admin records where on the Publishing page. If a photo in a package is later withdrawn, the package reads **Needs rebuilding**; use **Rebuild without withdrawn photos**.

## Numbers

**Metrics** (from Overview) shows coverage by category, review and how photos are used. Each number explains how it is counted. **Search photos and videos** finds anything from your venue.
