# Naba-Lens for staff

A short guide to the jobs you do in Naba-Lens. The words in **bold** are the words on your screen. For the full reference, see the older [staff guide](../usage-staff.md).

Naba-Lens keeps your venue's **album**: one **slot** for every dish, drink, space and facility that needs a photo. Your job is to add photos from your phone's gallery and say which slot each one is for. A manager then looks at each photo.

## The three tabs

- **Album**: your venue's slots, grouped by place. The top line says how many slots are still empty, for example "41 slots still empty".
- **Add photos**: opens your phone's photo picker.
- **Me**: **Your photos**, **Your month**, **Notices**, and **Help and account**. **Log out** is on Me on a phone and at the top of the screen on a computer.

## Four different things

Each stage has its own word on screen. One stage doesn't mean the next has happened.

| Stage                    | What you see                                                       | What it means                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your photo is on its way | **Saved on this phone**, **Sending**, **Checking**                 | The photo is still travelling from your phone. Keep Naba-Lens open until it reads Waiting.            |
| Sent, not yet checked    | **Waiting**                                                        | Your manager hasn't looked at it yet. A Waiting photo doesn't fill its slot.                          |
| Checked                  | **In the album**, **Kept for reference**, **Retake**, **Not used** | A manager decided. Only **In the album** fills a slot.                                                |
| On a website or menu     | Nothing on your screens                                            | Admins export photos and record where they are published. In the album doesn't mean it is online yet. |

## Add photos from your gallery

Naba-Lens has no camera of its own. Take photos with your phone's camera as usual, then add them from your gallery.

1. Tap **Add photos** (or **Add photos from your gallery** on Album). Your phone's photo picker opens.
2. Pick up to 20 photos. They start saving as soon as you pick them.
3. Match each photo to a slot (see the next section). Each photo is sent as soon as you choose its slot. There is no separate Send button.
4. At the end, Naba-Lens shows what happened, for example "**3 slots filled**" and "**3 photos for The Bell**". Each one shows as **Waiting** until your manager has looked at it. Tap **See the album**.

If a photo didn't send, the end screen says so; open **Add photos** to send it again. If you can't add photos at all, see **If you can't add photos** under Help and account on Me.

## Match a photo to its slot

For each photo, Naba-Lens asks **Which empty slot is this?** and shows **Recent** and **Suggested** slots. To find another, use **Search all empty slots**.

- **Several photos of the same thing:** after you choose a slot, Naba-Lens asks **Same slot for the next photos too?** Choose **All _n_ left**, **Just this one**, or tap the last photo of the same thing to group them.
- **A slot that already has a waiting photo** reads **Waiting · another photo is welcome**. You can still add yours.
- **A note for your manager:** tap **Add a note**. It is optional.
- **A dark or blurred photo:** Naba-Lens tells you, but you can still send it.
- **Amenities** (a garden, step-free entrance and so on) are not matched here. Open the amenity in the album and answer its question first.

### When you're not sure

- **I'm not sure**: your manager will choose the slot. Use it when you can't tell which dish or place it is. You don't need to guess.
- **Match later**: keeps the photo with you, without a slot. It waits on Album under **Match later** ("_n_ photos to match"); open it and match each one, or say it is **Not for a slot**.
- **Not for a slot**: the photo is shared with the team, with no slot. Naba-Lens asks what it shows.

## Fill a slot from the album

1. On **Album**, choose your place under **Where are you?**, or open a place.
2. Tap an empty slot (**Add**). Your gallery opens for that slot, so there is nothing to match.
3. On a place, **Add photos for these _n_ slots** fills its empty slots one after another.

Open a filled slot to see **What the photo must show**, **The photo in the album** and **Your photos here**.

**Asked for**, on Album, lists slots your manager has asked for, with their note and, when there is one, a **Needed by** date. Tap **Add photos for this**.

## Retake

When a manager asks for another photo, the slot reads **Retake** with the reason, for example "Too dark".

1. Tap the retake row on Album, the **Retake** button in Your photos, or the notice.
2. The slot page says **You were asked to retake this** and the reason.
3. Take a new photo with your camera, then tap **Add a photo for this slot** and choose it from your gallery.

The new photo goes to your manager as **Waiting**.

## Your photos (on Me)

**Your photos** lists everything you've added. Use **Show** to pick **Needs attention**, **Sending**, **Waiting** or **Reviewed**.

| Word on your photo     | What it means                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting**            | Your manager hasn't looked at it yet.                       |
| **In the album**       | Accepted for public use. It fills its slot.                 |
| **Kept for reference** | Kept for the team, not for public use. The slot stays open. |
| **Retake**             | Another photo is wanted. The reason is shown.               |
| **Label corrected**    | Your manager moved it to the right slot. Nothing to do.     |
| **Not used**           | Not used.                                                   |
| **Withdrawn**          | You took it back before it was checked.                     |

What you can do from a photo:

- **Change slot**: you put it in the wrong slot, and it hasn't been checked yet.
- **Fix date taken**: the date is wrong.
- **Withdraw**: only before it has been checked. It leaves the album, and this can't be undone.
- **Ask to remove**: for a photo that is In the album or Kept for reference. You can't delete it yourself; you give a reason and an admin decides. The photo then reads **Removal asked for**.

## Answer an amenity question

Some slots are questions, such as whether the venue has a pool table.

1. Open the amenity on Album, or from **Other jobs**.
2. Answer **Yes**, **No**, **Not applicable** or **Couldn't establish**.
3. Add what the screen asks for under **Needed for Yes**, then tap **Submit finding**. Use **Save for later** to come back to it.

Some answers, such as Wi-Fi, are confirmed by your manager before they count.

## Notices

Retakes, jobs given to you and photos that didn't send appear under **Notices** (Me › Help and account) and as a number on the Me tab, never as pop-ups. Choose what to be told about under **What to tell you about**. Nothing is sent between 21:00 and 08:00 at your venue.

## Help

- **Photo guide** (on Me): how to photograph a dish in four angles, and what files Naba-Lens accepts.
- **Your managers** (Me › Help and account): who to ask.
- **Your month** shows your own progress. Only you, your manager and admins see it.
