# Staff guide

Naba-Lens keeps your venue's **album**: one **slot** for every dish, drink,
space and facility that needs a photo. You add photos from your phone's
gallery and say which slot each one is for. A manager then looks at each
photo. The words in **bold** are the words on your screen.

Managers have their own [manager guide](usage-manager.md); admins have the
[admin guide](usage-admin.md).

## Four different things

Each stage has its own words. One stage doesn't mean the next has happened.

| Stage                        | What you see                                                       | What it means                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sending: still on its way    | **Saved on this phone**, **Sending**, **Checking**                 | The photo is still travelling from your phone. Keep Naba-Lens open until it reads Waiting.                           |
| Sent, not yet checked        | **Waiting**                                                        | Your manager hasn't looked at it yet. A Waiting photo doesn't fill its slot.                                         |
| Checked by a manager         | **In the album**, **Kept for reference**, **Retake**, **Not used** | A manager decided. Only **In the album** (accepted for public use) fills a slot. Kept for reference leaves it empty. |
| Used on a website or listing | Nothing on your screens                                            | Admins export photos and record where they are published. In the album doesn't mean it is online.                    |

## Sign in and leave safely

Open `/login`, enter your invited address in **Email**, then choose
**Send sign-in link**. The email (subject "Your Naba-Lens sign-in link and
code") holds a link and a 6-digit code. At **Check your email**, either open
the link on the same device, or type or paste the code into **Sign-in code**
and choose **Sign in with code**. The link and the code each work once, and
using one ends the other. **Check your email** is a neutral response: it does
not confirm access or delivery, and never creates an account.

- **Send a new code** sends a fresh email with a new link and code. You can
  ask again after a short wait (the button shows the seconds left). Use the
  newest sign-in email you have; older ones may stop working.
- **Use a different email** goes back if you typed the wrong address.
- For **That sign-in link has expired**, choose **Send a new link**.

Invitation emails stay link-only: open the invitation link to accept it, then
sign in later with either the link or the code.

Staff land on **Album** (`/staff/tasks`). If **Account unavailable** appears,
ask an admin; that screen has **Sign out**. Use **Log out** when you finish,
especially on shared devices: on a phone it is on **Me**, on a tablet or
computer it is in the top bar.

## Find your way around

On a phone the tab bar has three tabs: **Album**, **Add photos** and **Me**.
On a tablet or computer the same places are in the top bar, with **Add
photos** as a button.

- **Album** (`/staff/tasks`): your venue's slots, grouped by place, and what
  your manager has asked for. While a manager has asked you for another
  photo, the Album tab shows how many retakes wait.
- **Add photos**: opens your phone's photo picker, then **Match your photos**.
- **Me** (`/staff`): **Your photos** first, then **Your month**, your work
  rows (**Edit work details**, **How Naba-Lens works**, **Photo guide**, **Our
  menu**, **Photos**, **People**, and **Other jobs** when any wait on you),
  then **Help and account**. Unread notices show as a number on the Me tab.

Naba-Lens has no camera of its own. Take photos with your phone's camera as
usual, then add them from your gallery. Your phone's picker may offer its
camera too.

## Start here and your work details

A short welcome (`/staff/tasks/welcome`) explains what happens to a photo,
then offers **Add your work details**, an optional profile photo, and the
photo buttons. Until you add your first photo or dismiss it, **Album** shows
a **Start here** row that opens it. You can always open it from **Me** with
**How Naba-Lens works**; **Back to Me** returns you.

**Edit work details** on **Me** (`/staff/profile`) asks **What do you do at
[venue]?**. Choose one main job (for example **Head chef** or **Waiter /
server**), any **Other areas you work in** (optional), or **Other** with a
short title, then **Save**. **Skip for now** leaves it blank. Your main job
decides which places and dishes come first; search still covers everything,
and it never labels a photo for you or changes what you can do. Only you and
admins see your work details.

## Album: what your venue still needs

**Album** is your home. Top to bottom:

1. **The Railway album** (your venue's name), then how many slots are still
   empty, for example **41 slots still empty**. The bar under it shows in the
   album, waiting and empty, with the numbers written under it ("65 in · 7
   waiting · 113 in all"). Slots marked **Not here** are not counted.
2. **Start here**, for a new starter.
3. A **retake** row, when a manager asked you for another photo: the slot
   and the reason. Choose it to add a new photo with the slot already chosen.
4. A row such as **2 photos didn't finish**, when photos stopped on their
   way. Open it to send them again; nothing is sent twice.
5. **Asked for**: the slots your manager has asked for, with their brief
   and, when dated, **Needed by** (then **Needed** once the date has passed).
   Yours come first, marked **For you**. **Add photos for this** opens your
   gallery for that slot. Nothing here is ever overdue.
6. **Where are you?** and the places at your venue (for example **The bar**,
   **Kitchen pass**, **Garden**), each with its number of empty slots. Album
   opens on the place you last chose, else the one that fits your work areas
   or has the most empty slots. Until your manager places each part of the
   menu, every slot is under **Not placed yet**.
7. **Empty at the bar** and up to six empty slots there, with **All 8** (the
   place's total) to open the place.
8. **Match later**, when you have photos not matched to a slot yet ("3
   photos to match").
9. **Other jobs**, when a check or venue question waits on you.
10. One main button, **Add photos from your gallery**.

Each slot shows its state with a frame, an icon and a word: **Add** (empty),
**Sending**, **Waiting**, **Retake**, **Needs checking**, **In the album** and
**Not here**. Tap an empty dish or place slot to add its photo straight from
your gallery; tap an amenity slot to answer its question first; tap a filled
slot to open its page.

A slot marked **Optional** (a bottled or canned drink nobody needs a photo
of) is not counted: a photo is welcome, but it never shows as empty. Tap it to
open its page and add one.

You see a colleague's photo only once a manager has accepted it. Until then
the slot just says how many photos are waiting ("1 waiting").

### A place

Open a place (**All 8**, or its chip) to see every slot there by section,
each section with its "filled of total" count, and the place's count ("12
empty of 40").

- **Show** filters by state: **Needed**, **Waiting**, **In the album**,
  **Retake or needs checking** and **All**. Your choice is remembered on this phone.
- **Category** filters by **Food**, **Drinks**, **Spaces**, **Facilities** and
  **Other** (only those found in the place), or **Everything**.
- Filters never change the counts. With nothing to show, the page says **No
  slots here match these filters. Choose another filter.**
- **Add photos for these _n_ slots** fills the place's empty slots one after
  another; with one left it reads **Add a photo for the empty slot**.
- **Report a change** tells your manager about any slot here (see below).

### A slot's page

A filled slot opens its own page with **What the photo must show** (the
brief), **The photo in the album**, how many photos wait for a manager, and
**Your photos here** with what happened to each. If you were asked for
another photo, it says **You were asked to retake this** and the reason. Use
**Add a photo for this slot** (or **Add another photo**) to send one from your
gallery, and **Report a change** to tell your manager something has changed.

### Report a change

When a dish or space no longer looks like its photo, choose **Report a
change**. Pick **What has changed?** (**It's no longer served**, **It looks
different now**, **The space isn't available**, **The name or label is
wrong** or **Something else**), add **Anything to add** if it helps, and
choose **Send to your manager**. Nothing on the album or the menu changes
until your manager checks it. If you already told them, the dialog says so.

## Add photos

1. Tap **Add photos** (or **Add photos from your gallery** on Album). Your
   phone's photo picker opens. Pick up to 20 photos; they start saving at
   once.
2. **Match your photos** shows one photo at a time (**Photo 1 of 6**) and the
   venue they go to (**For The Railway**). If you switch venue while it is
   open, it says so and asks again for the photos it can't keep.
3. For each photo it asks **Which empty slot is this?**, with **Recent** and
   **Suggested** slots for your place. To find another, use **Search all
   empty slots**; it knows the menu's other names and forgives small typos.
   A slot that already has a photo waiting reads **Waiting · another photo is
   welcome**.
4. Each photo is sent as soon as you choose its slot. There is no separate
   Send button. The count at the top says how many are left ("2 not matched
   yet").

### Use one answer for several photos

After you choose a slot, Match asks **Same slot for the next photos too?**.
Tap the last photo of the same thing (**Photo 4 too**), choose **All _n_
left**, or **Just this one**. Nothing is grouped unless you ask.

### When a photo is not a slot

- **I'm not sure**: **Your manager will choose the slot.** Use it when you
  can't tell which dish or place it is. You don't need to guess.
- **Match later**: keeps the photo with you, without a slot. It waits on
  Album under **Match later**, where you match each one or say it is **Not
  for a slot**. A photo left there for 7 days also goes to your manager, who
  can choose its slot.
- **Not for a slot**: the photo is shared with the team with no slot. Match
  asks **What does it show?**.
- A dish that isn't on the menu: when search finds nothing, Match offers to
  suggest it to your manager as a special (**It's food** or **It's a
  drink**). For that photo, then choose **I'm not sure**.
- **Amenities** (a garden, a step-free entrance) are not matched here. Open
  the amenity in the album and answer its question first.

### Notes and hints

- **Add a note** opens **Note for your manager (optional)**. Nobody has to
  write one.
- Match may say a photo looks dark or blurred, or is small. **You can still
  send it**; a small photo may be kept for reference.
- A photo you sent before reads **Sent before: it won't count twice**.

### What happened (the end summary)

When every photo has an answer, the end screen says what happened, for
example **3 slots filled** and **3 photos for The Railway**, with a chip for
each slot. It also says how many are **With your manager** (I'm not sure),
under Match later or shared with no slot, and how many didn't send ("1 photo
didn't send. Open Add photos to send it again."). Each sent photo shows as
**Waiting** until your manager has looked at it. Tap **See the album**.

Without a signal, the end says **1 photo saved on this phone** and **They send
when the signal returns.** Keep Naba-Lens open.

### Sending words

Each photo you add shows one of these, on Match, its slot tile and **Your
photos**:

| Word                    | What it means                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Saved on this phone** | No signal yet. It sends when the signal returns.                                |
| **Sending**             | It is on its way.                                                               |
| **Checking**            | It arrived and is being prepared.                                               |
| **Waiting**             | It is with your manager.                                                        |
| **Sent**                | It arrived, but its slot wasn't saved with it. Match it again from Match later. |
| **Couldn't finish**     | It arrived but couldn't be finished. Open **Add photos** and choose **Retry**.  |
| **Not sent**            | It didn't leave your phone. Open **Add photos** to send it again.               |

## Retake

When a manager asks for another photo, the slot reads **Retake** with the
reason, for example "Too dark".

1. Tap the retake row on Album, **Retake** on the photo in **Your photos**, or
   the notice.
2. The slot page says **You were asked to retake this** and the reason.
3. Take a new photo with your camera, then tap **Add a photo for this slot**
   and choose it from your gallery.

The new photo goes to your manager as **Waiting**.

## Me

Me's main **Add photos** opens your gallery and then Match, like the tab; the quieter **Add a video** beside it opens the older Add sheet, for a video or a file Match doesn't take.

### Your photos

**Your photos** is the first section on Me and lists every photo you've
added. **Show** picks one list, each with its count:

- **Needs attention**: retakes, photos with no slot yet, and photos that
  couldn't be finished.
- **Sending**: photos still being prepared.
- **Waiting**: sent, and waiting for a manager.
- **Reviewed**: checked by a manager.

Each row shows the photo, its slot, when it was sent and taken, the venue it
went to when that is not your current one, and the outcome:

| Word on your photo     | What it means                                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Waiting**            | Your manager hasn't looked at it yet.                       |
| **In the album**       | Accepted for public use. It fills its slot.                 |
| **Kept for reference** | Kept for the team, not for public use. The slot stays open. |
| **Retake**             | Another photo is wanted. The reason is shown.               |
| **Label corrected**    | Your manager moved it to the right slot. Nothing to do.     |
| **Not used**           | Your manager decided not to use it.                         |
| **Withdrawn**          | You took it back before it was checked.                     |

A photo with no slot reads **No slot yet** (match it from Album, under Match
later) or, after **I'm not sure**, **With your manager, who will choose its
slot.** A photo sent twice reads **Already sent before: it doesn't count
twice**.

What you can do from a photo:

- **Retake**: opens its slot with the reason.
- **Change slot**: you chose the wrong slot and it hasn't been checked yet.
  It then waits for a manager under its new slot. A photo sent to another
  venue says to switch to that venue first.
- **Withdraw**: only before it has been checked. **Withdraw this photo?** says
  it leaves the album, your manager won't check it, and this can't be undone.
- **Fix date taken**: **When was it taken?** in UK time, or **I don't know**.
- **Ask for a correction**: once it has been checked, if it's matched to the
  wrong slot or the date is wrong. Your manager checks it; the photo stays as
  it is until then.
- **Ask to remove**: for a photo that is In the album, Kept for reference
  or Label corrected. You can't delete it
  yourself: give **Why should it be removed?** and an admin decides. The photo
  then reads **Removal asked for**, and Me tells you if the admin can't remove
  it.

### Your month

**Your month** shows your own monthly photo target and how far you've got,
plus how many slots in the album your photos fill this month and how many
outdated photos they replaced. **Only you, your manager and admins see
this.** Nobody is ranked. **See your month** opens **Monthly photos**
(`/staff/tasks/month`), with **Add photos for this month** and **Past
months**.

Accepted photos you labelled (dishes, drinks and venue areas), and photos you
sent to the month, count. Each photo counts once, for the month its upload
started; one accepted after its month ended is marked **Accepted late**.

### Help and account

- **Your venues**: where you work, as "Working at The Railway · Staff". If
  you work at more than one venue, a venue switcher appears here; Album,
  Match and Change slot then use the venue you choose.
- **Notices** (`/staff/notifications`): retakes, checks given to you, slots
  you were asked for, label corrections and photos that didn't send, newest
  first, with **New** on unread ones and **Mark all as read**. Each opens
  where it leads. Under **What to tell you about**, choose **Retakes asked
  for**, **Checks given to you** and **Photos that didn't send**. Notices are
  never pop-ups, and nothing is sent between 21:00 and 08:00 at your venue.
- **Your managers**: who to ask about a photo, a slot or your account.
- **If you can't add photos**: photo access on your phone, file types and
  sizes.

**Edit profile** changes **Display name** and **Profile photo** (up to 4 MB),
then **Save changes**. Your admin controls job title, team, venue and access.

## Photo guide

**How to photograph a dish** (`/staff/tasks/guide`, from **Photo guide** on
**Me**) shows the **Every shot** checklist, a safety note and four example
angles in order (**45° angle**, **Overhead**, **Close-up**, **Side view**),
each with a short tip. Tap an angle for a full-screen view with **Previous**
and **Next**. The pictures are examples only, not your menu.

## Answer an amenity question

Some slots are questions, such as whether there is a step-free entrance.
Open the amenity on Album, or from **Other jobs** (`/staff/tasks/other`),
where a check given to you shows **Needs capture**, or **Needs recheck** with
the manager's reason.

1. Read the question, **What to capture** and **Required evidence**.
2. Answer **Yes**, **No** or **Not applicable** (some venue questions also
   offer **Couldn't establish**). **No** and **Not applicable** need a
   **Reason**.
3. Add **What you found** and any **Limitations** in plain words.
4. Under **Evidence**, add what the screen asks for under **Needed for Yes**.
5. Choose **Submit finding**, or **Save for later** to come back to it.

For **guest Wi-Fi**, cover the password and join QR code before any photo,
then tick **I have covered the Wi-Fi password and join QR code**. A Yes also
needs the **Wi-Fi test** notes, which only you, your manager and admins see.
Some answers are confirmed by your manager before they count. Your draft
stays on this phone until it is sent. If sending fails, **Try again** sends
the same finding once; if someone else changed the check, choose **Reload**.

## Our menu

**Our menu** (`/staff/menu`, on **Me**) lists your venue's active menu by
section, with how many dishes have an accepted photo. A dish shows **Has an
accepted photo**, **Still needs a photo**, **Your photo is waiting for
review** or **Being checked after a menu change**. A dish your manager needs
first shows **Needed by** a date.

- **Search the menu**, choose a section (or **All sections**), and switch
  between **All dishes** and **Still needs a photo**. **Clear search** starts
  again.
- **Add photos of this dish** adds photos already labelled with that dish.

## Photos and People

- **Photos** (`/staff/community`): accepted photos from colleagues, newest
  first. Choose **Filters**, set **Venue**, **Team** or the kind of media,
  then **Apply filters**; **Clear filters** resets them. Open one to see who
  took it, and **Open profile** for their profile.
- **People** (`/staff/people`): search colleagues by **Name**, **Venue** or
  **Team**, then **Find people** and **View profile**.

Colleagues see your photos only once accepted, as previews without location
details. Unreviewed photos stay between you and your managers.

## Add labels

Me's **Add a video** button opens the older Add sheet with **Choose
from your photos** (and, on some devices, **Take a photo**). Photos start
saving at once. Optionally fill in the sheet's question:

- **What's in these photos?** Search the menu and pick a dish or drink.
  It applies to every photo in the sheet; **Adjust** changes one photo.

Choose **Done** at any time; each row ends in **Saved**, **Saving…** or
**Needs attention** with one action (**Retry**, **Remove**, **Fix labels** or
**Retry labels**).

To label a photo later, open it in your portfolio on Me and choose **Add
labels** (or **Edit labels**). **Needs labels** on Me counts your photos with
no label; choose **Label these _n_ photos** to label them together.

- Search the menu of the venue where the photo was sent, not your current one
  if you have moved.
- A dish not listed? Use **Item not on the menu?** and type its name; an admin
  reviews it. Don't pick an item that isn't really in the photo.
- A label says what is in the photo. It is not a claim about allergens,
  dietary suitability, price or availability, and not permission to publish.

When labels were kept but couldn't be added to a photo (often because the
menu changed), **Me** lists it under **Needs attention** with **Fix labels**
and **Discard labels**.

## Delete your media

Open your own photo or video on Me and choose **Delete**. A photo a manager
has accepted can't be deleted: Naba-Lens says it is kept, and you can ask an
admin to remove it (**Ask to remove** in Your photos). Downloaded copies
cannot be recalled.

## Older Manage tools

Grants made before manager accounts existed still open **Manage**
(`/staff/manage`) for that venue: on a phone it is the first row on **Me**.
Those tools are described in the [manager guide](usage-manager.md); an admin
can convert the account to a manager account.

Capture safely and follow your organisation's privacy and permission rules.
See [capture guidance](operations.md).
