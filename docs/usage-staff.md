# Staff guide

1. Open `/login`, enter your invited email and choose Send sign-in link. Open the email link. If it expired, request another. Ask an admin if access is blocked; an uninvited address does not create an account.
2. Your `/staff` portfolio shows your own uploads. Choose Upload photos or videos and select up to ten files. Keep the page open while transferring; two files transfer at a time. Photos may be up to 20 MiB and videos up to 200 MiB. Follow [capture guidance](operations.md).
3. Watch each file's transfer and processing result. Retry a failed file rather than uploading successful files again. Ready media appears in your portfolio and the admin library automatically. Processing is a technical check, not marketing approval. A missing/inactive venue assignment prevents upload; ask an admin to correct it.
4. Use Edit profile to change your display name or photo (up to 5 MiB). An admin manages your job title and venue.
5. Open an item to preview it. For removal, choose Remove and confirm. This removes it from both views. Previously issued links may last briefly and downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Video preview depends on your browser and codec. An unsupported preview does not mean the original is lost. HEIC phone and deployment checks remain release requirements. Sign out when finished, especially on a shared device.
