# Staff guide

## Sign in and leave safely

Open `/login`, enter your invited address in **Email**, then choose
**Send sign-in link**. The email (**Your Naba-Lens sign-in link and code**)
holds a link and a 6-digit code. At **Check your email**, either open the
link on the same device, or type or paste the code into **Sign-in code** and
choose **Sign in with code**. The link and the code each work once, and using
one ends the other. **Check your email** is a neutral response: it does not
confirm access or delivery, and never creates an account.

- **Send a new code** sends a fresh email with a new link and code. You can
  ask again after a short wait (the button shows the seconds left). Use the
  newest sign-in email you have; older ones may stop working. If you go back
  or reload the page within that wait, the code step comes back instead of a
  second email being sent.
- **Use a different email** goes back to **Sign in** if you typed the wrong
  address.
- For **That sign-in link has expired**, choose **Send a new link**.

Invitation emails stay link-only: open the invitation link to accept it, then
sign in later with either the link or the code.

Staff land on **Home** (`/staff/tasks`). If **Account unavailable** appears,
ask an admin; that screen has **Sign out**. Use **Log out** when you finish,
especially on shared devices: on a phone it is on **Me**, from a tablet or
computer it is in the top bar.

## Find your way around

On a phone, the TabBar at the bottom has **Home**, **Photos**, **Add**,
**People** and **Me**. **Add** is the raised button in the middle. From a
tablet or computer the same sections are in the top bar, with **Add photos**
in place of **Add**; venue managers also see **Manage** there. On a phone,
**Manage** is the first row on **Me**.

- **Home** (`/staff/tasks`): what to do next, your month and your tasks.
- **Photos** (`/staff/community`): ready photos and videos from colleagues
  across Lapen Inns.
- **Add**: take or choose photos and upload them.
- **People** (`/staff/people`): find colleagues and see what they've shared.
- **Me** (`/staff`): your profile, work details and your own photos and
  videos (your portfolio) and, for venue managers, **Manage**.
- **Our menu** (`/staff/menu`, linked from Home and **Me**): which dishes and
  drinks on your venue's menu still need a photo.

## Start here and your work details

New staff see a **New to Naba-Lens?** card on Home with **Start here**. It
opens a short welcome (`/staff/tasks/welcome`) that explains your portfolio
and what happens to a photo, then offers **Add your work details**, an
optional profile photo, and the photo buttons (**Choose from your photos**
first, **Take a photo** second). Choosing a photo there, or **Skip for now**,
hides the card from Home. It also stays away once you have added photos. To
read the welcome again at any time, open **Me** and choose **How Naba-Lens
works**; **Back to Me** returns you.

**Work details** (`/staff/profile`, or **Edit work details** on **Me**) asks
**What do you do at [venue]?** and shows your name and venue. Choose one main
job (for example **Head chef**, **Bartender** or **Waiter / server**), any
**Other areas you work in** (optional), or **Other** with a short title of up
to 40 characters, then **Save**. **Skip for now** leaves it blank; you can
come back to it from **Me**. If your admin already set a job title, the form
may suggest a match for you to check.

Your main job sets the question Home asks (for example **What did you cook
today?** or **What did you make at the bar today?**) and which dishes come
first when you label. Search still covers the whole menu, and your job never
labels a photo for you or changes what you can see or do. Only you and admins
see your work details. Your admin still sets your job title, team and venue.

## Home: monthly photos and venue tasks

**Home** shows what you need to photograph and how your month is going. Dates
are UK time.

- At the top, a question based on your work details (**What did you make or
  capture today?** if you have none) with **Choose from your photos** (several
  at a time) and **Take a photo**. Both open the same upload queue as **Add**.

- **To do** lists what needs you first: **Needs another photo** requests from
  your manager, with the reason and **Send another photo**, then tasks that are
  **Overdue** or due soon, each with **Send photos**. With nothing to do it
  says **Nothing to do right now.**
- **Your setup isn't finished** appears until you have a name, a venue and a
  team (Front of house or Back of house). A profile photo is optional. An
  admin assigns venue and team; your manager sets your monthly target. Until
  all are set your month shows **Needs setup**, which never counts as
  complete.
- The month card (**Your photos for November 2026**, for example) shows your
  target and progress, such as **2 of 5 accepted**. **Target met** appears once
  accepted photos reach the target. Until your manager sets a target it says
  **Your target appears here once your manager sets it.**
- **All tasks** lists the venue tasks assigned to you, overdue first, then by
  due date. **Overdue** is shown for follow-up only: you can still send photos.
- **Our menu** shows how many menu items at your venue have an accepted
  photo, such as **12 of 40 menu items have an accepted photo**, with **Browse
  our menu**. This is a different measure from your monthly target and the
  venue's section coverage. Before your venue has a menu it says **Menu photos
  will appear once your venue’s menu is set up**; you can still add photos to
  your portfolio.
- **From your colleagues** shows a few of the newest photos. **See more
  photos** opens **Photos**.

Next to these, Home repeats the rule: **Ready photos are already in Photos and
on your profile. Review decides whether they count, not whether they are
shared.**

### Send photos to a task

1. Open a task and read **What to photograph**.
2. Choose **Send photos** (or **Send more photos**). Use **Choose from your
   photos** or **Take a photo** to add new ones, or pick your own ready photos
   from this venue under **Or send photos you've already added**; a photo that
   can't be sent says why under it. You can send up to 10 at a time. New
   photos are selected for you one by one, as soon as each is ready: you
   don't wait for a slow file, and photos you already selected stay selected.
3. Choose the send button, which counts your selection ("Send 2 photos").

Only tasks assigned to you accept photos. Your team (FOH or BOH) can help your
manager decide who to assign, but it never gives access to a task. To count
photos toward your target without a task, choose **Add photos for this month**
on the month card. It opens **Monthly photos** (`/staff/tasks/month`), which
also lists **Past months**, read-only.

If sending fails, **Try again** resends the same request, so nothing is sent
twice. If the task changed while you were working, choose **Reload**.

### Photo guide

A food menu-section task shows **How to photograph it** between **What to
photograph** and **On this section**: four example angles in order (**45°
angle**, **Overhead**, **Close-up**, **Side view**), each with a short tip.
The first angles are marked **Suggested** to match the section's photo target
(all four from a target of 4). Dish buttons switch the example; tap an angle
for a full-screen view with **Previous** and **Next** (arrow keys or a swipe
also work). **See the full photo guide** (`/staff/tasks/guide`) adds the
**Every shot** checklist and the safety note. You can open it at any time, even
when your venue has no menu tasks yet: **How to photograph a dish** on Home's
add-photos card and on **Monthly photos**, or **Photo guide** on **Me**. The pictures are examples only, not your menu,
and your manager still decides which photos are accepted. Drinks sections and
amenity, service and venue tasks do not show the guide.

### What counts toward your monthly target

A photo counts once a reviewer accepts it and all of these are true: you
uploaded it yourself, it is a photo rather than a video, it was uploaded at
your venue, and you uploaded **and** sent it in the same UK calendar month.
A photo's month is set when its upload starts, so an upload started just
before midnight at the end of the month belongs to that month.

- Each photo counts once. Labels, retries and sending it again add nothing.
- A photo accepted after its month ended still counts for the month you sent
  it, marked **Accepted late · counts for October 2026** (for example).
- An older photo can still complete a task. It shows **Counts for the task
  only, not your monthly target.**
- Deleting a photo reopens any task it completed. Deleted during the month you
  sent it, it also stops counting toward that month; earlier months keep their
  accepted totals and mark the photo as removed.
- Photos a manager adds to a task count for the task only, and stay yours.

### Review results and replacement photos

Each photo you send is **Submitted**, then **Accepted** or **Needs another
photo** with a reason. Decisions are final. To answer a request for another
photo, choose **Send another photo** on Home or on the task; it says **The
photos you send now replace this one.** Take a new photo or choose one of
yours, then send it. The earlier photo and its reason stay in your history,
and the earlier photo stays in Photos and on your profile.

Ready photos are already in Photos and on your profile before anyone reviews
them. Review decides whether a photo counts, not whether it is shared, and it
is not publishing clearance. You cannot review photos, set targets or change
assignments.

If you move venue, your old venue's tasks stop taking new photos; accepted
photos and past months stay credited to the old venue.

### Amenity checks

When your manager assigns you an amenity question (for example step-free
entrance, parking or guest Wi-Fi), it shows on Home under **Amenity checks**
with **Needs capture**, or **Needs recheck** and the manager's reason when they
asked you to look again. Amenity checks are separate from monthly photo
targets: they never count toward them.

Open a check to see the question, **What to capture** and **Required
evidence** (**Photo**, **Video**, **Photo or video** or **Photo and video**).

1. Answer **Yes**, **No** or **Not applicable**. **No** and **Not
   applicable** need a **Reason**; evidence is optional for them.
2. Add **What you found** and any **Limitations** in plain words.
3. Under **Evidence**, choose **Choose from your photos** or **Take a photo**
   to add a photo or video, or tick ones you already uploaded. Each new upload
   is ticked for you as soon as it is ready, one by one, without waiting for
   the others. A line under **Evidence** says whether
   the evidence rule is met; a **Yes** cannot be sent until it is (a photo is
   not enough where a video is required).
4. Choose **Submit finding**. There is no review step: the finding becomes the
   venue's current answer straight away and you go back to Home.

For **guest Wi-Fi**, cover the Wi-Fi password and the join QR code before you
take any photo or video, then tick **I have covered the Wi-Fi password and
join QR code**. A **Yes** also needs the **Wi-Fi test** notes (where you
tested, the test date, download and upload Mbps). These are internal: only
you, your venue manager and admins see them.

Your answer, text, ticked evidence and Wi-Fi notes are kept as a draft on this
device until the finding is sent, so a failed upload, a lost connection or a
reload loses nothing. If sending fails, choose **Try again**: the same finding
is sent again and never recorded twice. If someone else changed the check in
the meantime, choose **Reload**; your draft stays.

## Manage (venue managers only)

Venue managers now have their own account type and workspace (see the
[manager guide](usage-manager.md)). This section covers grants made before
that change, which an admin can still see as **Legacy staff grant**: they keep
working until the admin converts the account.

With such a grant for your current venue, **Manage**
(`/staff/manage`) then appears in the top bar on a tablet or computer, with
**Overview**, **Review**, **Checklist**, **Menu**, **Our menu** and **Team**. On a phone,
open **Me** and choose **Manage {venue}** at the top. When photos are waiting,
Home's **Review photos** card is a shortcut. Without the grant the page
explains that the tools are for venue managers. A grant ends if you move venue,
your account is deactivated or an admin revokes it; that takes effect on your
next page load or action.

- **Overview**: the month at your venue: photos to review, checklist progress,
  menu coverage and the team's targets.
- **Review**: submitted photos wait here, oldest first. Choose **Accept**, or
  **Ask for another photo** and give a reason (up to 500 characters). You may
  review your own photos; the page warns first, and admins can see each
  **Self-review**. If someone else decided first, the page shows the latest
  state instead of overwriting it.
- **Checklist**: suggested items (exterior and signage, entrance, toilets,
  accessible toilet, outdoor seating, parking, dog facilities, family
  facilities and more) start as **Needs setup**. For each one, confirm whether
  it applies, its photo target (1 is only a starting suggestion), guidance,
  due date and who is assigned, then **Save and confirm**. For amenities,
  record whether it is present in **Amenity facts** first; **Needs
  verification** blocks confirming it. **Does not apply** needs a reason; the
  item stays visible as **Not applicable** and is not counted. Photos never
  prove an accessibility or dog-friendly claim. Use **Add a checklist item**
  for anything specific to your venue.
- **Attach existing photos** adds ready photos already taken at your venue,
  by you or an active colleague, to a checklist item, optionally accepting them
  at once. They count for the venue only; the photographer keeps authorship
  and gets no monthly credit.
- **Menu**: an admin activates menus. Choose **Start from this menu** to add
  one item per menu section, set each one up, then **Confirm menu coverage**.
  A section completes on its number of distinct accepted photos; dish labels
  stay optional. When a new menu is activated you see **Menu changed — review
  coverage**. Nothing moves automatically: for each old section choose which
  accepted photos to carry and the new section to carry them to, with a reason,
  or **Carry nothing and close it**. Menu coverage is not current until every
  old section is done.
  Staff see the dish [photo guide](#photo-guide) on food sections (and
  sections of unset or "other" kind), never on drinks sections.
- **Our menu** (`/staff/manage/menu/items`): accept photos for individual
  dishes and drinks, a separate measure from section coverage and monthly
  targets. Labelled ready photos taken at your venue wait here, oldest first,
  with how long each has waited; you can also open one dish to see **All
  photos of this dish**. Each photo shows its dish, variant, the venue it was
  uploaded at and who took it. No task or assignment is needed.
  - **Accept for this dish** counts the dish as covered (tick **Use as the
    dish's main photo** to make it the one shown). The uploader sees
    **Accepted for menu use** on **Me**.
  - **Ask for a better photo** needs a reason the uploader can read.
  - **Undo acceptance** needs a reason; if it was the main photo, another
    accepted photo takes its place or the dish needs a photo again.
  - **Make main photo** swaps which accepted photo is shown.

  Only photos labelled with a dish appear; you cannot label a colleague's
  photo, so ask them to label it first. A photo can be accepted for each dish
  it clearly shows. You may review your own photos: the page says so and the
  decision is recorded as a self-review that admins can see. If someone else
  decided first, the page shows the latest state instead of overwriting it.
  When a new menu is activated, a dish whose name, section kind or variant
  changed shows **Check after a menu change** until someone reviews it again;
  dishes no longer on the menu drop out of the total. If the photo's option
  (variant) is no longer offered, the dish's review screen shows it with
  **No longer on the menu** and only **Undo acceptance**. An undone photo
  stays out of **To review** until its uploader labels it again.

- **Team**: set each person's monthly target and optional due date within the
  month. Changing a target already set for this month needs a reason. Past
  months are **Read-only**; only an admin can correct them. **Prepare this
  month** copies each person's last target at this venue, adds the suggested
  checklist items and flags anyone new or without a team. It also runs every
  hour on its own and is safe to repeat.

Being a manager does not let you invite or deactivate people, change roles,
venues or teams, publish menus, download originals or delete colleagues'
photos.

## Photos and People

**Me** (`/staff`) remains your own profile and upload workspace.

- Open **Photos** (`/staff/community`) to see ready photos and videos from
  active staff across the organisation, newest first. Choose **Filters**, set
  **Venue**, **Team** (Front of house / Back of house) or **Media**, then choose
  **Apply filters**. Use **All venues**, **All teams** and **Photos and videos**
  for the full view, or **Clear filters**. With no results you see **Nothing
  matches these filters.**
- Open a photo to preview it and see who took it and the venue at upload.
  **Open profile** takes you to that colleague's profile and **Shared
  photos**. Use **Load more** for older uploads.
- Open **People** (`/staff/people`) to search colleagues by **Name**, **Venue**
  or **Team**, then choose **Find people**. Each card shows the person's
  profile details and ready-upload count. Choose **View profile**; use **Next
  people** when there are more, and **Clear search** to start again.

People uses a colleague's current venue; Photos uses the venue recorded when
the file was uploaded. Team filters use the colleague's current assigned team.
Staff without a team assignment still appear under **All teams**.

Photos and People are available to active signed-in staff. Inactive colleagues
are not listed, and their media is unavailable to peers; admins retain access
to their ready media. Uploading, processing, failed and deleted media are not
shown in Photos or colleague profiles. Your own profile on **Me** can still
show your incomplete uploads so you can manage them.

Colleague profiles provide viewing, without account editing, label editing,
deletion or **Download original**. Names, profile photos, job titles, venues,
teams and ready-upload counts are visible; account email, access-management
details and original filenames are not part of the colleague view.

Colleagues see your photos as previews without embedded details. **When a
colleague plays one of your videos, they receive the original file**, including
any embedded details such as the location where it was recorded, and they can
save it. If a video should not carry that, turn off location for your camera
before recording or do not upload it.

## Update your profile

On **Me**, choose **Edit profile**, change **Display name** or **Profile
photo**, then **Save changes**. Photos can be up to 4 MiB (shown as **4.0 MB**).
Your admin controls job title, team, venue and access. Ask them to fix a
missing or inactive venue if uploads are paused.

## Add photos from any page

Choose **Add** in the TabBar (or **Add photos** in the top bar). On a phone the
**Add photos** sheet opens first:

- **Take a photo** opens your phone's camera.
- **Choose from your photos** picks up to 10 at a time from your phone's
  library.

**Photos start saving as soon as you pick them.** There is no separate Upload
button. If your account can't add photos yet, the sheet says **You can't add
photos yet** and why. Home, the welcome, **Me**, **Our menu** and task screens
all use this one Add.

### Saving your photos

After you pick, the **Saving 3 photos** sheet (**Saving 1 photo** for one)
shows a row for each file. Each photo can be up to **20 MiB**, each video
**200 MiB** (shown as **20 MB** and **200 MB**).

1. Optionally say what's in them under **What's in these photos?
   (optional)**: search your venue's menu and pick a dish or drink. It applies
   to every photo in the sheet. If one photo is different, choose **Adjust**
   on that photo to change just that one.
2. Choose **Done** at any time. You don't have to wait: uploads and labels
   carry on while this page stays open. **Add more** picks more files into the
   same sheet.

Each row ends in exactly one of three words:

- **Saved**: the photo is processed and its labels, if you chose any, are
  stored on it. A dish photo says, for example, **Saved · Onion Bhaji ·
  waiting for manager review**.
- **Saving…**: still working. The line under it says **Uploading 40%** or
  **Processing**. With a weak or lost connection it carries on when the signal
  returns; nothing is sent twice.
- **Needs attention**: something needs you. The line says why, with one
  action:
  - **Sign in again, then tap Retry.** You were signed out. Sign in, then
    choose **Retry**; finished photos are safe.
  - **This file can't be used: {reason}.** The file itself can't be
    processed (for example it is corrupt). Choose **Remove** and pick a
    different file; retrying the same file won't help. Its labels show **Not
    saved (photo not saved)**.
  - **The menu changed. Check the dish.** The dish you chose was changed or
    removed from the menu while the photo was saving. Choose **Fix labels**,
    pick the dish again from the current menu and save.
  - **Labels not saved yet.** The labels couldn't be sent (usually the
    connection). Choose **Retry labels**.

The footer counts what is left, for example **1 photo still saving labels**.
Labels you choose are kept on the server from the moment the photo starts
saving (or from when you choose them, if that is later), and are added as soon
as the photo is ready. If you discard a photo's labels, the confirmation says
**Uploads carry on; only these labels are discarded.**

The photo itself needs this page open until it is saved. While files are still
uploading, or labels haven't reached the server yet, **Log out** and links that
leave the staff pages ask first, for example **Leave now? Labels for 1 photo
haven't saved yet.** Closing or reloading the tab gets your browser's own
warning. If the tab is closed, reloaded or killed while a photo is still
uploading, that photo only finishes if you pick the same file again on the
same device, signed in as you, within about 20 hours: it carries on where it
stopped and its kept labels are added. After that, pick it again as a new
photo and choose its labels again. **Me** doesn't show uploads that are still
in progress: its **Needs attention** list shows only problems, and a photo
that finished appears in your portfolio.

On task screens and amenity checks the sheet does not ask for labels. Each
photo is handed to the screen as soon as it is ready, and you still choose
**Send photos** (or **Submit finding**) there yourself.

## Upload from Me

On **Me**, **Upload photos or videos** opens the same Add with **Choose from
your photos** and, on some devices, **Take a photo**. The picker accepts JPEG,
PNG, WebP and HEIC/HEIF photos, plus MP4, MOV and WebM videos; server
validation can still reject a file.

**Me** also has **Edit work details**, **How Naba-Lens works** and **Our
menu** rows.

### Needs attention

When labels were kept for a photo but couldn't be added to it (most often
because the menu changed before the photo finished saving, or the photo's
upload failed), **Me** lists the photo under **Needs attention**, with its
thumbnail and the reason. This list stays until you deal with it, even after
you leave Add or sign in on another day. It lists only problems: photos still
uploading are not on it.

- **Fix labels** opens the labels you chose against the current menu. Dishes
  still on the menu are kept; any that are gone say **No longer on the menu**
  and can be removed. Choose the right dish and save. The photo is never
  uploaded again.
- **Discard labels** asks first: **Discard these labels?** with **The photo
  isn't changed; only these labels are discarded.** Choose **Discard labels**
  to drop them, or **Keep labels**. Discarding never changes the photo; if it
  finished saving, you can label it later.
- **Labels not saved yet.** with **The photo didn't finish saving. These
  labels are kept and go on it when it does.** means the upload stopped for a
  reason that can pass (for example the server was busy). Choose **Retry** on
  that photo in **Add** if it is still there. If it isn't, or Retry doesn't
  help, discard these labels, then pick the photo again from **Add** and
  choose its labels again. **Fix labels** changes the kept labels; they still
  wait for the photo.
- If the photo's upload added the labels before your discard reached the
  server, nothing is discarded: the row turns **Saved**, says **These labels
  were already saved on the photo.** and shows them. Choose **Fix labels** to
  change them, or **Keep labels** to close the row.
- If the file itself can't be used, the row says **This file can't be used:
  {reason}.** (or **no reason was given** when the server didn't say why) and
  its labels **Not saved (photo not saved)**. The only action is **Discard
  labels**; to add that photo, pick it again from **Add**.

## Add labels (optional)

Labels help admins find your photos and videos later, and a photo needs a
dish label before a manager can accept it for that dish. They are optional for
your portfolio: skipping them never affects the upload, and every upload
appears in the admin library once ready, labelled or not.

Each photo passes three separate milestones:

1. **Saved to your portfolio**: as soon as it uploads. Colleagues see it in
   Photos.
2. **Ready to label**: say which dish or drink it shows, so it can be found.
3. **Accepted for menu use**: a manager chose it to show a dish on your
   venue's menu. **Me** lists these photos under **Accepted for menu use**
   with the dish, however long ago you uploaded them. If a manager asks for a
   better photo instead, **Me** shows it under **Better photo requested** with
   the dish and their note.

- The easiest time to label is while saving: **What's in these photos?** in
  the **Saving** sheet (see above). To skip, just choose **Done**.
- To label later, **Me** counts your ready photos without a label under
  **Needs labels**; choose **Label these photos** (or **Label this photo**) to
  label them together. Those photos are marked **Needs labels** in your
  portfolio. You can also open any of your ready photos or videos and choose
  **Add labels** or **Edit labels**.
- Cards in your portfolio and in the admin library show the dishes each photo
  is labelled with, so you can find it without opening it.
- Dishes from your main job's areas come first, but search covers the whole
  menu.
- Search the menu of the venue the file was **uploaded at** (not your current
  venue, if you have moved). Results show the section, so "Chicken" under Korma
  and "Chicken" under Masala are different items.
- For a dish with options (for example **Which Momo?**), **Not specified** is
  chosen until you pick one, so the dish is always saved.
- Add up to four content types (Finished dish, Preparation, Plating, Drinks,
  Kitchen, Team, Venue, Event) and an optional short note (280 characters).
- Dish not listed? Use **Item not on the menu?** and type its name; an admin
  reviews it. Don't pick an item that isn't really in the photo.
- With several files, the **Common to all {n} photos** (or **videos**, or
  **files** for a mix) choices apply to every file;
  open a file's **Adjust** panel to change just that one.
- If someone else changed the labels, or the venue's menu changed while you
  were editing, the dialog tells you and keeps your edits so you can retry.

A label describes what is in the file. It is not a claim about allergens,
dietary suitability, price or availability, and not permission to publish.

## Our menu

**Our menu** (`/staff/menu`) lists your current venue's active menu, grouped
by section, with how many menu items have an accepted photo overall and per
section. Food and drinks count; other sections don't. A dish shows **Has an
accepted photo**, **Still needs a photo**, or **Being checked after a menu
change**.

- **Search the menu** by dish or drink, choose a section (or **All
  sections**), and switch between **All dishes** and **Still needs a photo**.
  **Clear search** starts again.
- **Take a photo of this dish** opens your camera; **Choose from photos**
  opens your photo library instead. The photo starts saving straight away,
  already labelled with that dish: it shows as a chip (for example **Onion
  Bhaji**) that you can remove with **Remove Onion Bhaji** if the photo shows
  something else. Then choose **Done**. That's two taps in the app: **Take a
  photo of this dish**, then **Done**.
- A dish counts once a manager accepts a photo labelled with it for menu use.
  An upload or a label alone does not count. Your photos are saved to your
  portfolio either way.

With no venue, an inactive menu or a menu without dishes, the page says so;
you can still add photos to your portfolio.

## View or delete your media

**Me** shows your own uploads, newest first, including incomplete states such
as **Uploading** or **Failed**. Open a ready tile to preview; use **Load
more** for older items. **Preview unavailable in this browser** can appear for
unsupported video codecs. Format acceptance does not guarantee playback on
every device.

To remove an item, open it and choose **Delete**. **Delete this photo?** (or
**Delete this video?**) says what changes, for example:

> It's removed from your profile and from Photos. It stops counting for Chef's Specials and your November target, and the task reopens. When backups are enabled, copies are retained indefinitely for recovery. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Choose **Keep photo** to cancel or **Delete** to confirm. The same file is
removed from your profile, Photos and the admin library. Reload Photos or a
colleague's profile to update it. Existing signed links may work until expiry;
saved copies remain.
When the original-media archive is enabled, deletion does not erase its private
recovery copy. The owner has chosen indefinite archive retention; local/staging
